@AGENTS.md

This is a **lab homepage** (decided 2026-10-16), not a personal one: the hydrology and water resources lab led by Daeha Kim (Department of Civil Engineering, Jeonbuk National University), built from the al-folio v1.x template and served at <https://daehakim-waterlab.github.io>. `AGENTS.md` (imported above) describes how the theme works: layouts, includes, and styles live in the `al_*` gems, not here. [`README.md`](README.md) maps each menu item to the files behind it and lists common editing tasks.

## Menu structure

Home · Research · People · Publications ▾ · Projects · Teaching ▾ · Blog · Join us

- **Home** (`_pages/about.md`, title "Home"): a hero box whose lab name and department are the page's HTML header (`site.title` and `subtitle`), next to a text-free illustration (`assets/img/home/hero-art.webp`, cropped from a Google Gemini illustration made at the owner's request); side by side from 768px, stacked below. Then the lab introduction, the five latest news items with an "All news" link (the News page `/news/` lists every item grouped by year), and the five latest blog posts with an "All posts" link. No address block on Home; contact details are in the footer. The current name "Hydrology & Water Resources Lab" is an interim description: change `title:` (and `short_title:`) in `_config.yml` when the official name is chosen.
- **No photos** of the PI or members (owner's decision).
- **People, PI section:** a short bio that summarizes the career path (institutions since graduate school in 2005), then Education, Professional experience, and Editorial service. The owner prefers a modest page: no honors or awards, society memberships, or meeting participation (removed 2026-10-17). The full CV is kept elsewhere; do not transcribe it.
- **Research**: six themes (evapotranspiration/CR/Budyko; droughts and flash droughts; adaptation and decision-centric assessment; ungauged basins and machine learning; climate services for agriculture; wetlands), each with three representative publications linked by DOI, listed oldest to newest. Prefer full research articles over meeting summaries.
- **People**: PI bio and CV summary. Members and alumni are rendered from `_data/people.yml`; a section appears only when it has entries.
- **Publications ▾**: dropdown with three sub-pages, one per BibTeX entry type in `_bibliography/papers.bib`: `@article` → Peer-reviewed Journal Articles, `@incollection` → Book Chapters, `@techreport` → Technical Reports. The parent page `/publications/` links to the three. Each list is one continuous reverse-chronological list without year headings (`scholar.group_by: none`, owner's preference); order comes from `papers.bib`, newest first. Conference presentations are deliberately not listed (owner's decision, 2026-10-16).
- **Projects**: rendered from `_data/projects.yml`; split into Ongoing/Completed by comparing each project's `end` year with the build year.
- **Teaching ▾**: dropdown with the four courses in `_teachings/` (Engineering Hydrology, Hydraulics, Physical Hydrology (graduate), Statistical Hydrology). Each has an English and a Korean paragraph (`summary_en`, `summary_ko`). Online textbooks are separate Jupyter Book sites: setting `textbook:` in a course's front matter replaces "Online textbook — coming soon" with the link.
- **Blog**: al-folio blog with categories `paper-highlights` and `notes`, shown in the menu since the first post (2026-10-17); Home lists the three latest posts. The Blog page links only categories that already have posts. Figures in posts are generated by code: put the script in `scripts/blog/<post-date>-<name>.py` (run from the repository root; `scripts/` is excluded from the built site), commit both the script and the PNG it writes to `assets/img/blog/`, and link the script on GitHub at the end of the post. Add `{: style="width: 100%; height: auto;" data-zoomable="" }` after a Markdown image so it fits the column and can be tapped to enlarge on phones.
- **Join us**: What we expect / What I commit / How to apply, in English only (Korean removed at the owner's request, 2026-10-17).
- Contact details are in the site footer (`footer_text` in `_config.yml`) and on Home, not in the menu.

## Content rules

- **English is the main language.** Korean appears only in the Teaching course descriptions and in the short Korean summary at the end of a blog post (for busy Korean readers).
- **Public contact details only:** the lab address, the office phone, and the JBNU email address, as already set in `_config.yml`, `_pages/about.md`, and `_data/socials.yml`. Never add a mobile number or a personal email address. Do not copy contact details into other files, including this one.
- **Publications list (`papers.bib`):** published and in-press papers only, never papers under review or in revision. This rule applies to the publication list only (owner's decision, 2026-10-17). Other pages, such as Research, may cite manuscripts under review or in revision, marked "(in revision)" or "(under review)" with no DOI; currently the Kim and Kim wetlands paper (*Journal of Hydrology*, in revision). Add such a paper to `papers.bib` once it is accepted. Mark in-press papers with `note = {In press}`. Journal articles carry a verified DOI; the owner's name (`Kim, D.`) is highlighted through `scholar.last_name`/`first_name` in `_config.yml`. A co-author with the same surname and initial is written with a full given name (for example `Kim, Doyoung`) so that only the owner is highlighted.
- **People:** names, roles, and degrees only; never students' email addresses or photos. Everyone listed on the lab's previous site (hydroclimatol.com) was approved for listing by the owner on 2026-10-16; ask the owner before adding anyone else.
- **No placeholder text** such as "[Placeholder]" or "coming soon" lists. If information is missing, leave the item out. The single exception is "Online textbook — coming soon" on course pages, which is intentional.
- **No copyrighted third-party material:** no figures from papers, publisher images, or abstracts copied from publishers. Use the owner's own figures and photos.
- **No large files in the repository** (slides, videos, datasets such as netCDF). Link them from Google Drive. `.gitignore` blocks common large formats.
- Google Scholar and ORCID links stay commented out in `_data/socials.yml` until the owner provides them.

## How to work in this repo

- Build and check locally before pushing (see README). CI runs `bundle exec jekyll build`, then PurgeCSS, then deploys; after deploy, `broken-links-site.yml` fails on any broken **internal** link, so every local link must resolve. Links to the blog are wrapped in `{% if site.posts.size > 0 %}` while it has no posts.
- `deploy.yml` only runs when files matching its `paths` list change; `**.scss` was added (2026-10-18) after style-only pushes were silently not deployed. If you add a new kind of source file, add it there too.
- `upgrade-check.yml` runs `bundle exec al-folio upgrade audit`; keep the `al_folio` contract keys in `_config.yml` (see [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#the-v1-config-contract)).
- `_includes/hook/bib.liquid` is a site hook that the theme's bib layout calls; it is not an override of a gem file. Prefer config, data, and page front matter (such as `_styles`) over overriding gem files.
- **Theme overrides** (acknowledged in `.al-folio-overrides.yml`; after a gem update run `bundle exec al-folio upgrade overrides audit` and re-check them):
  - `_includes/header.liquid`: lab name in the top bar on every page; `site.short_title` ("HWR Lab") replaces it beside the expanded desktop menu (992px and up) and below 360px.
  - `_includes/news.liquid`, `_includes/latest_posts.liquid`: dates as "Jun 2026" on one line, no fixed-height scroll box, Home shows the `announcements.limit` (5) latest news items, "All news"/"All posts" links on Home. Every new publication gets a news item. News items are one-sentence, text-only `inline: true` entries; no photos (owner's decision, 2026-10-18).
  - `assets/css/main.scss`: the theme's entry point plus `@use "site";`, which loads `_sass/_site.scss`.
- **Site CSS** is in `_sass/_site.scss`: opaque top bar, hamburger menu kept up to 991px (in `@layer components`, because the theme's `!important` rule lives in that layer), phone typography (16px, line height 1.6) and spacing, a smaller back-to-top button, and blog figure/caption sizing. `#back-to-top` is created by JavaScript, so it is safelisted in `purgecss.config.js`.
- **Mobile checks:** before pushing layout changes, take Playwright screenshots at 375, 390 and 1280px in light and dark mode (the dev dependency `@playwright/test` is already in `package.json`).
- The template's sample content and the CI workflows that tested it (`unit-tests`, `visual-regression`, `prettier`, `lighthouse-badger`, `render-cv`, `update-tocs`) were removed. `test/` remains only because `package.json`/`package-lock.json` are kept as upstream so that `npm ci` works.
- GitHub Pages must serve the `gh-pages` branch (Settings → Pages), not `main`: GitHub's built-in Jekyll build cannot load the `al_folio_core` theme. The root `.nojekyll` file (listed under `include:` in `_config.yml`) is copied into the build so Pages serves it as-is.
- Active workflows are pinned to `runs-on: ubuntu-24.04` because `ubuntu-latest` moves to Ubuntu 26 on 2026-10-19; revisit once `ruby/setup-ruby` supports it.
- `assets/json/resume.json` is a stub kept because `jekyll_get_json` in `_config.yml` reads it.

## Gem version pins

`Gemfile` pins every `al-*` gem to an exact released version in `group :al_folio_plugins`, and `_config.yml` lists the same gems under `plugins:`. A plugin present in only one of the two is inert.
