Three students presented their M.S. theses on 17 December 2024:

- **Seoung Cheol Lee**: a performance comparison of conceptual, machine-learning and hybrid modelling approaches for rainfall–runoff simulation in ungauged basins
- **Eunji Kim**: a comparative performance evaluation of topography-based wetland extent prediction methods in South Korea
- **Seokwoo Im**: evaporation and drought assessment using the complementary evaporation principle
