We study how water moves between the land and the atmosphere, and how to manage it when the climate and society are both changing.

Our group in the Department of Civil Engineering at Jeonbuk National University works on evapotranspiration and the land water balance, droughts and flash droughts, climate change adaptation for water infrastructure, streamflow prediction in ungauged basins, climate services for agriculture, and wetlands. Read more on the research page, see our publications, or find out how to join us.
